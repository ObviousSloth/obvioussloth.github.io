SlothsClips

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who this covers

SlothsClips is a personal tool used only by its operator. It has no public users and does not collect data from visitors to this website.

2. What data SlothsClips handles

  • Account authorisation: access tokens for the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts, plus basic profile information such as account ID and display name, used to upload videos.
  • Stream content: video, audio and public chat messages from live streams of creators who gave permission, used to find highlights and make clips.
  • Post statistics: views and likes of published clips, used to see which clips perform well.

3. How data is stored

All data is stored on the operator's own computer and storage. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Raw stream recordings are deleted after processing; finished clips are kept.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Videos are only sent to the platform they are published on.

5. Google API data

SlothsClips' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the security settings of TikTok, Instagram or Google. Stored tokens and data are then deleted. Chat messages and other stream data can be removed on request.

7. Contact

For questions or removal requests: github.com/ObviousSloth
