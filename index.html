SlothsClips

SlothsClips is a personal tool that turns highlights from live streams into short vertical videos with captions, and publishes them to the SlothsClips pages on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

Clips are only made from streamers who have given permission. Every clip credits the original streamer and links to their channel.

SlothsClips is not offered to the public. It only connects to accounts owned by its operator.
